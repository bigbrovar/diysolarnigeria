## What this wiki is

It is a collection of experience and opinion shared by members of the DIY Solar Nigeria Telegram group, gathered by topic so it is easier to find. Some pages also point to manufacturers' documents and other outside sources.

## What it is not

- **It is not a reference guide or a standard.** It does not replace the manuals for your equipment, the electrical code that applies where you live, or the advice of a qualified professional.
- **It is not a design for your home.** Every installation is different. A cable size, breaker rating or setting that suited one member's system may be wrong, or dangerous, for yours.
- **It is not guaranteed to be correct, complete or current.** Members sometimes disagree, people make mistakes, and products, prices and rules change. Each page says when the discussions it draws on took place.

## The risks are real

Solar and battery systems involve high DC voltages, very large battery currents and mains electricity. Mistakes can cause electric shock, burns, fire, explosion, damage to property and death.

- Have mains wiring, earthing and battery connections done or checked by a qualified electrician.
- Follow the manufacturer's instructions for every device, and its limits.
- If you are not sure, stop and ask someone qualified.
